1. Abstract & System Premise

The digital computational economy is witnessing an unprecedented convergence between electrical grid capacity and machine learning inference throughput[span_53](start_span)[span_53](end_span). Both industries suffer from market fragmentation: AI foundation model providers sell tokens via retail SaaS markups with rigid billing tiers, while regional electrical grids face duck-curve curtailment, renewable intermittency, and slow multi-month balancing cycles[span_54](start_span)[span_54](end_span).

e-Cache introduces a unified, decentralized clearinghouse deployed on Base (Ethereum Layer 2)[span_55](start_span)[span_55](end_span). As an institutional aggregator—conceptually the "Costco for AI Compute"—e-Cache purchases upstream inference throughput at bulk wholesale discounts, packages capacity into standardized consumer units ($ePT), and routes captured gross margins directly into Power Coin ($PWC) automated market buybacks[span_56](start_span)[span_56](end_span).

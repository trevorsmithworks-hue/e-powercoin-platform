What is e-Cache Power Coin?

Think of e-Cache Power Coin ($PWC) as a digital battery. In the same way that a dollar bill represents money, a Power Coin represents physical energy and computer power. We call this the "Electrodollar."

Right now, Artificial Intelligence (AI) and large computers use massive amounts of electricity. At the same time, solar panels and wind turbines sometimes produce more electricity than we can use, which goes to waste.

Our company, HUBALUBA, fixes this problem. e-Cache Power Coin is a subsidiary protocol of HUBALUBA. We buy up cheap, extra electricity, store it in batteries, and use it to power AI computers. We use the blockchain (the Base L2 Network) to keep a perfect, unchangeable receipt of this energy. That receipt is the $PWC token.

How the Tokens are Divided (The 80/20 Split)

To make sure the project is fair, safe, and trustworthy for the public, the total supply of Power Coins is strictly divided into two pieces:

80%

The Public Liquidity Pool

80% of the value generated by our energy system goes directly back to the public market. This ensures that regular people can buy, sell, and trade $PWC freely on the Base network. By putting 80% out to the public, it prevents the company from hoarding all the tokens and crashing the price.

20%

The Locked Company Treasury

20% is held by the parent company (HUBALUBA) to pay for servers, batteries, and developers. However, these tokens are cryptographically locked. They are locked completely for 12 months, and then slowly released over 36 months. This proves the founder cannot run away with the funds.

Official Whitepaper

1. Abstract & System Premise

The digital computational economy is witnessing an unprecedented convergence between electrical grid capacity and machine learning inference throughput. Both industries suffer from market fragmentation. Regional electrical grids face "duck-curve" curtailment, renewable intermittency, and slow multi-month balancing cycles.

e-Cache Power Coin ($PWC) introduces a unified, decentralized clearinghouse deployed on Base (Ethereum Layer 2). As an institutional aggregator, e-Cache purchases upstream inference throughput at bulk wholesale discounts, packages capacity into standardized consumer units, and routes captured gross margins directly into $PWC automated market buybacks.

2. The Electrodollar Concept

Money traditionally represents debt or gold. The Electrodollar concept means that $PWC represents 1 Kilowatt-hour (kWh) of stored electrical battery arbitrage or its equivalent in AI compute processing power. It is a functional cryptocurrency anchored to the physical world.

3. Dual-Rail Economic Engine

Rail 1: Machine Intelligence
e-Cache pre-procures bulk compute across major AI clusters. Consumers purchase non-expiring processing bundles. The margin spread generates programmatic surplus.

Rail 2: Physical Energy
Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) interface directly with the clearinghouse. When electricity prices plunge, power is routed to localized compute nodes or settled on-chain.

4. Tokenomics & The 80/20 Split

All net margins captured are autonomously settled through strict smart contract rules.

80% Automated Liquidity Buybacks:
80% of net arbitrage proceeds are executed directly against Base decentralized liquidity pools. Purchased tokens are permanently burned or allocated to protocol staking vaults, applying consistent deflationary buy pressure.

20% Founder & Protocol Reserve:
The remaining 20% is assigned to the HUBALUBA protocol treasury. To ensure strict long-term alignment, this allocation is locked with a 12-month cliff followed by a 36-month linear drip.

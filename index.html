e-Cache Protocol Specification

The Dual-Rail Settlement Standard for Wholesale AI Inference Arbitrage, Distributed Energy Clearing, and Algorithmic Liquidity on Base.

1. Abstract & System Premise

The digital computational economy is witnessing an unprecedented convergence between electrical grid capacity and machine learning inference throughput. Both industries suffer from market fragmentation: AI foundation model providers sell tokens via retail SaaS markups with rigid billing tiers, while regional electrical grids face duck-curve curtailment, renewable intermittency, and slow multi-month balancing cycles.

e-Cache introduces a unified, decentralized clearinghouse deployed on Base (Ethereum Layer 2). As an institutional aggregator—conceptually the "Costco for AI Compute"—e-Cache purchases upstream inference throughput at bulk wholesale discounts, packages capacity into standardized consumer units ($ePT), and routes captured gross margins directly into Power Coin ($PWC) automated market buybacks.

2. Dual-Rail Economic Engine

Rail 1 • Machine Intelligence

Wholesale AI Compute Arbitrage

e-Cache pre-procures bulk compute across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Llama clusters. Consumers purchase non-expiring $ePT bundles using $PWC, ETH, or USDC. The margin spread generates programmatic surplus.

Gross Margin = Package Price ($ePT) - Bulk Upstream Cost
Rail 2 • Physical Energy

Distributed Energy Clearing

Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and solar micro-inverters interface directly with the clearinghouse. When electricity prices plunge or curtailment occurs, power is routed to localized compute nodes or settled on-chain.

Target Parity: 1 PWC ≈ 1 kWh Storage Arbitrage Utility

3. Programmatic Revenue Split & 20% Founder Vesting

All net arbitrage margins captured through the epower-inference-proxy are autonomously settled through strict smart contract rules.

Automated Liquidity 80%

Spot Market Buybacks on Base

80% of net arbitrage proceeds are executed directly against Base decentralized liquidity pools ($PWC/$WETH and $PWC/$USDC). Purchased tokens are permanently burned or allocated to protocol staking vaults, applying consistent deflationary buy pressure tied directly to worldwide AI inference consumption.

Founder / Protocol Reserve 20%

36-Month Linear Vesting Schedule

The remaining 20% is assigned to the HUBALUBA protocol treasury to fund ongoing operations, capital infrastructure, and upstream wholesale pre-purchases. To ensure strict long-term alignment, this allocation is locked with a 12-month cliff followed by a 36-month linear drip (~2.77% released monthly).
